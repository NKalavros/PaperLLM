# Academic Talk Summarizer: Speaker Guide

## Quick Start

As a speaker, you evaluate how well AI models understood your talk. Your expert evaluation helps improve AI performance on academic content.

### Using the Speaker Interface

1. **Select your talk** from the dropdown
2. **Choose number of questions** to review (default: 5)
3. **Load Questions & Answers** - you'll see random audience questions about your talk
4. **Rate each answer**:
   - **Preferred Answer**: Which model better captured your points? (optional)
   - **Quality Scores**: Rate each model 1-10 based on technical accuracy (optional)
5. **Submit Ratings** - these contribute to the separate "Speaker Leaderboard"

### Viewing Results (Leaderboard Tab)

- **Audience Leaderboard**: Shows how general audience rated the models
- **Speaker Leaderboard**: Shows expert evaluations from speakers only

## Tips

- Focus on technical accuracy when rating
- Consider whether the AI captured nuanced points from your talk
- Rate based on how well you'd want this summary to represent your work
- Even partial ratings are valuable - you don't need to complete all fields
   - They contribute to the separate "Speaker Leaderboard"
   - The system flags these as speaker evaluations using the `is_speaker` parameter

### Why Speaker Ratings Matter

- You have unique insight into whether LLMs correctly understood your work
- Your ratings help identify which models best handle technical academic content
- The platform tracks speaker vs. audience ratings separately to compare perspectives
- These will be compared with LLM as a judge post-hoc to evaluate the accuracy of this approach in this setting

### Viewing Results (Leaderboard Tab)

- **Audience Leaderboard**: Shows average quality scores by difficulty level from all audience ratings
- **Speaker Leaderboard**: Shows scores from speaker evaluations only

## Understanding the Results

### Leaderboard Metrics
- **Mean ± SEM**: Average quality score ± standard error of the mean
- **Easy vs Hard**: Performance breakdown by question difficulty level
- **All**: Overall performance across all difficulty levels
- **N**: Number of rating comparisons collected for statistical tests

### Your Contribution

Your expert evaluation as the content creator helps:
- Validate whether LLMs accurately understand technical academic content
- Identify which models perform better at capturing nuanced scientific points
- Compare expert vs. general audience perspectives on AI performance
- Improve future AI systems for academic applications

The platform helps identify which LLMs best understand and communicate academic content, benefiting both researchers and the broader academic community through systematic evaluation of AI capabilities in academic contexts.
